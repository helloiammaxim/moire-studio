Motion

Press ▶ next to any setting to animate it. It moves between From and To, taking Time seconds per cycle. Ping-pong goes there and back smoothly. Loop runs one way and starts over: a 180° span on Rotation or a span of 1 on Shift A/B makes it seamless. Stopping puts the setting back to From.

Export

SVG keeps the two layers as separate groups (layer-a, layer-b), one path per line, ready for Illustrator, Figma or a plotter.
